Voxshelf

Free audiobooks. No ads, no account.

Voxshelf plays over 21,000 public domain audiobooks read by LibriVox volunteers: classics, mysteries, poetry, history and more.

  • Find something goodBrowse by subject, see what's popular, or let it surprise you.
  • Pick the best recordingMany books have several readings. See which one listeners liked.
  • Listen offlineDownload a book for the plane, the trail or the subway.
  • Pick up where you left offYour spot is saved, and the sleep timer stops when you drift off.

Made possible by LibriVox

Every recording comes from LibriVox, where volunteers around the world read public domain books aloud. If you enjoy the books, consider volunteering or donating to LibriVox.

Audio and cover art are hosted by the Internet Archive. Book ratings come from Open Library. Voxshelf is an independent app and isn't made or endorsed by LibriVox.

Free and open source

Voxshelf is free to use, with no ads, no tracking and no account. The code is open source under the GPL-3.0 license, so anyone can read it, improve it or build on it.
