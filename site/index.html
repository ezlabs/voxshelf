Made possible by LibriVox volunteers

Every recording comes from LibriVox, where thousands of volunteers around the world read public domain books aloud and give the recordings away. Voxshelf is simply a nice way to listen to them.

Audio and cover art are hosted by the Internet Archive, and book ratings come from Open Library. Voxshelf is an independent app and isn't made or endorsed by LibriVox.

Volunteer or donate to LibriVox

Free and open source

No ads, no tracking and no account, ever. The code is open source under the GPL-3.0 license, so anyone can read it, improve it or build on it.
